## What is GitHub?

**GitHub** is a website where developers store, share, and collaborate on code. Code on GitHub lives in **repositories** (or "repos"): project folders that also keep the full history of every change.

## Why do you need an account?

- **GitHub Copilot**, the main AI agent in this workshop, signs in with your GitHub account. The free plan, **Copilot Free**, is tied to your account.
- The starter project and this workshop guide are hosted on GitHub.

The account is **free**.

---

## Create a free account

1. Go to <https://github.com/signup>.
2. Enter your **email address**, a **password**, and a **username**.
   - Your username is public. Choose something professional, because you may use it for your portfolio.
3. Complete the verification puzzle.
4. Check your email for a **launch code** from GitHub and enter it on the page.
5. If GitHub asks you to choose a plan, choose **Free**.

> 🔐 **Recommended:** turn on **two-factor authentication (2FA)** to protect your account. GitHub may require it. Go to **Settings → Password and authentication** and follow the steps. See [GitHub's 2FA guide](https://docs.github.com/en/authentication/securing-your-account-with-two-factor-authentication-2fa/configuring-two-factor-authentication).

## Verify that your account works

1. Sign out of GitHub, then sign back in at <https://github.com/login>.
2. Click your **profile picture** (top-right) → **Your profile**. You should see your username.
3. Open the starter project: <https://github.com/IAMebonyhope/simple-expense-tracker>. You should see its files and README.

✅ **Success looks like:** you can sign in and view the starter repository.

## Important: Copilot through a company or school

If your GitHub account already gets Copilot through an **organization** (your employer, for example), you **can't** use Copilot Free on that account. The organization's settings control which features you can use, and an administrator may have turned off Copilot CLI.

If that applies to you, either check with your administrator that Copilot Chat and Copilot CLI are enabled, or use a **personal** GitHub account for the workshop.

> 🎓 **Students and teachers:** you may be eligible for free access to the paid **Copilot Pro** plan through [GitHub Education](https://github.com/education). This is optional. Copilot Free is enough for the workshop.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| No launch code email | Check your spam/junk folder. Wait a few minutes, then click **Resend**. |
| "Username is not available" | Try a variation, such as adding your surname or a number. |
| Can't sign in after enabling 2FA | Use the recovery codes you saved when you set up 2FA. See [GitHub's account recovery help](https://docs.github.com/en/authentication/securing-your-account-with-two-factor-authentication-2fa/recovering-your-account-if-you-lose-your-2fa-credentials). |
